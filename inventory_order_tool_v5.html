Jewish Holiday Peak Season Planner

Each holiday shows the Gregorian date, days until it arrives, and your peak purchasing window. Enable the holidays relevant to your business — the Order Analysis page will flag seasonal items and apply a demand multiplier based on prior year sales spikes.

Legend — Days Until Holiday

Under 60 days — Order immediately
60–120 days — Plan now
Over 120 days — Monitor

📋 How Seasonal Multipliers Work

When you upload a prior year dated sales file, the tool compares each item's sales during the holiday peak window vs. its annual average. If an item sold 2× more during Pesach week, the tool applies a 2× seasonal multiplier to the order quantity for the upcoming Pesach. This is combined with current year trend data.

⏳ Upload a prior year sales file (Item No · Date · QTY) in the sidebar to enable full seasonal multiplier calculations.
